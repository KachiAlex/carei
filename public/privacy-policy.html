Privacy Policy

Last updated: 28 August 2025

CAREi ("we", "us", "our") is an AI-powered care management platform operated by CAREi Ltd, registered in England and Wales. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data when you use the CAREi mobile application and web platform (the "Service").

UK GDPR Data Protection Act 2018 NHS DSPT Aligned

We are committed to protecting personal data in accordance with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

1. Data Controller

CAREi Ltd is the Data Controller for personal data processed through the Service. For data protection enquiries, contact our Data Protection Officer at: dpo@careiapp.com

2. Data We Collect

2.1 Data You Provide

  • Account data: Name, email address, phone number, role (carer, manager, family member)
  • Authentication data: Email and PIN for login; biometric data (fingerprint/face) processed locally on-device only, never transmitted to our servers
  • Care records: Voice notes, transcriptions, care observations, medication administration records (MAR), body map annotations, photographs
  • Visit data: Clock-in/out times, GPS location during active visits, visit notes and summaries
  • Compliance data: DBS certificate details, right-to-work documentation, training certificates

2.2 Data Collected Automatically

  • Device data: Device model, OS version, app version, unique device identifier
  • Location data: GPS coordinates during active visits only (for lone worker safety and visit verification)
  • Usage data: App interactions, feature usage, crash reports, performance metrics
  • Communication data: In-app messages between carers, managers, and family members

3. How We Use Your Data

  • Providing care management functionality (documentation, MAR, care plans, visit scheduling)
  • Ensuring lone worker safety through SOS alerts and live GPS tracking during visits
  • Maintaining CQC-compliant audit trails for medication administration and care delivery
  • Facilitating communication between carers, managers, and family members
  • Generating AI-powered transcriptions and care note summaries
  • Monitoring compliance (DBS, training, right-to-work)
  • Improving the Service through usage analytics and crash reporting
  • Meeting legal and regulatory obligations under CQC and NHS DSPT

4. Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contract: Processing necessary to provide the care management Service under our terms
  • Legal obligation: Compliance with CQC regulations, employment law, and data protection law
  • Vital interests: Lone worker safety and emergency SOS response
  • Legitimate interests: Service improvement, security, and fraud prevention
  • Consent: For optional features like push notifications and analytics

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share data only with:

  • Care agencies: Your employer (the care agency using CAREi) has access to your care records and compliance data
  • Family members: Designated family members may view care task summaries and visit information via the Family Portal
  • Emergency services: Location data may be shared with emergency contacts when SOS is triggered
  • Service providers: Cloud hosting, AI transcription services, and push notification providers — all under GDPR-compliant data processing agreements
  • Regulators: CQC, NHS, or law enforcement when legally required

6. AI Processing

Voice notes are transcribed using AI-powered speech-to-text. Audio is processed and deleted after transcription. Transcriptions are stored as care notes. AI is also used to:

  • Structure and categorise care notes
  • Generate care note summaries and handovers
  • Flag potential concerns (e.g., medication interactions)
  • Suggest care plan interventions

AI processing is conducted under GDPR-compliant agreements. No personal data is used to train third-party AI models.

7. Data Security

  • Encryption: All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256)
  • Authentication: JWT-based authentication with secure token storage on-device
  • Biometric login: Fingerprint/face data never leaves the device — processed by the OS secure enclave only
  • Screenshot prevention: FLAG_SECURE prevents screenshots and screen recording on Android
  • Clipboard guard: Clipboard is cleared when the app goes to background
  • Remote wipe: Lost or compromised devices can be remotely wiped
  • Auto-lock: App locks after period of inactivity
  • Access controls: Role-based access (carer, manager, family, super-admin)

8. Data Retention

  • Care records: Retained for the duration of the client's care plus 8 years (CQC requirement)
  • MAR records: Retained for 8 years per CQC regulations
  • Compliance data (DBS, training): Retained for duration of employment plus 6 months
  • Voice audio: Deleted immediately after transcription
  • Location data: Deleted 90 days after the associated visit
  • Account data: Deleted within 30 days of account closure

9. Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Be informed about how your data is used
  • Access your personal data
  • Rectify inaccurate data
  • Erasure ("right to be forgotten") — subject to legal retention obligations
  • Restrict processing
  • Data portability
  • Object to processing
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise these rights, contact dpo@careiapp.com

10. International Data Transfers

Data is stored on servers located within the United Kingdom and European Economic Area (EEA). Where any processing occurs outside the UK/EEA, appropriate safeguards (UK International Data Transfer Agreement or adequacy decisions) are in place.

11. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed at children under 18. CAREi is designed for adult care professionals and care recipients. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

12. Cookies and Tracking

The mobile app does not use cookies. The web platform uses essential cookies for authentication only. No third-party advertising or tracking cookies are used.

13. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify users of significant changes through the app or via email. The "Last updated" date above reflects the most recent revision.
